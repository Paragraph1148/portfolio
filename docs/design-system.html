02 · Legends

Type

You picked the keyboard over the serif. So mono carries everything structural — headings, labels, data, nav, buttons — because that is what a legend is. 03 · The component

Keycap

This is the answer to the boxy Mission Control problem. A keycap is still a rectangle, but it has a top face, a side wall, a cast shadow and a gap around it — so it reads as an object you could pick up, not a bordered div. At rest it's glass: translucent, blurred, with a specular rim, so the case reads through it. Move across one and two lights track you at once — a white specular sliding over the surface, and the red switch glowing up through it from below. Nothing on the site gets a flat outline.

07 · Before I build

Open

  • Katakana stays — it's the script for foreign words and names, which is exactly what this page is. Your own name renders リシャブ in it, and it's the convention the Red Samurai keycap sets use. Settled.
  • The Mission Control re-skin lands in automated-driving, which this session only has read access to. I'll deliver it as a patch you apply, unless you want me to attach the repo with push rights.
  • The glass costs a backdrop-filter per cap, which is cheap on a laptop and not free on a mid-range Android. On the real site I'll cap how many are on screen at once and drop the blur below a width threshold, keeping the rim and the backlight — those are nearly free and carry most of the effect.
